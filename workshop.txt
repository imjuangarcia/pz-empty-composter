version=1
title=Empty Composter
description=Right-click a composter, open [b]Gardening[/b] and pick [b]Empty Composter[/b] to throw out whatever compost is left inside, the same way you can empty a rain barrel.
description=
description=[h2]Why[/h2]
description=A sack takes compost 2.5 at a time. When less than that is left, there is no way to take it out, and a composter that still holds compost can't be picked up. This mod clears it.
description=
description=[h2]How to use it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click a composter that still has compost in it.
description=[*]Open [b]Gardening[/b] and pick [b]Empty Composter[/b], right below Get Compost. Your character walks over and empties it.
description=[*]Take out anything else inside, like worms, and pick the composter up as usual.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42 only. Tested on 42.22 in single player.
description=[*]Multiplayer: the server applies the change, the same way vanilla actions do. Not tested on a server yet.
description=[*]Lua only, no new items. Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
description=[*]Translations: English and Spanish.
description=[/list]
